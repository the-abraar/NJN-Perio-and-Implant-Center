FAQ

Common questions before your first visit.

What is gum disease?

Gum disease is inflammation or infection around the teeth. Warning signs include bleeding gums, swelling, bad breath, gum recession, and loose teeth.

How long does a checkup take?

Most routine checkups fit into one 45-minute appointment block. Some procedures may need a follow-up visit.

How do I prepare for my first visit?

Bring your ID, previous X-rays or prescriptions if you have them, and a short note about your symptoms or dental history.

Do you accept emergency walk-ins?

Please call the doctor first for emergency support so the clinic can guide you safely.

What payment methods are accepted?

The clinic accepts common local payment methods. Please confirm details with the assistant before your visit.
